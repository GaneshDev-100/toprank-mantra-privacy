Toprank Mantra is an Android app for UPSC Civil Services exam preparation. It gives you 10+ years of previous-year questions with answer reasoning, topic insights, and "Wormhole" links between related Prelims and Mains questions.

Free trial

Every new user gets a 24-hour free trial of Full Access — no card required — to evaluate the app before paying.

Full Access plans

  • The full previous-year question bank — Prelims GS1 & CSAT, Mains GS1–4, and Essay.
  • Answer reasoning and topic insights for every question.
  • "Wormhole" links connecting related Prelims-to-Prelims and Prelims-to-Mains questions.
  • An ad-free experience.
